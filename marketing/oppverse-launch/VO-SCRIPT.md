# Oppverse AI launch film: voice-over script

**Delivery:** warm, confident, understated. Think a calm product-launch narrator, not an advert.
- About 140 words per minute, lower-mid pitch.
- Let the pauses breathe, because the music does the hype.
- Say "Oppverse" as **OP-verse**. If the voice gets it wrong, type it as "Opp-verse".

## Clean script (paste this)

> Somewhere, an opportunity just opened.
> It's buried across fifty websites.
> And then... it's gone.
>
> Opportunity should find the right person.
>
> This is Oppverse AI.
>
> Build your profile once. Your experience, your goals, your eligibility, with a persona for every path.
>
> Jobs, fellowships, scholarships, grants and global programs. One universe.
>
> Every match is scored across five layers, and explained.
> Not eligible? It never reaches you.
>
> Ranked for you by fit, eligibility and deadline. Live.
>
> Save. Compare. Tailor. Track. Accepted.
>
> Stop searching everywhere. Start seeing what fits.
>
> Oppverse AI. Build your opportunity profile today.

## Best way to record

Generate **each numbered line as its own clip**, named `01.mp3` to `13.mp3`, and send them all. Each clip is then dropped at its exact start time (`python3 mix.py --vo ...`), with the music dipping under the voice. That's more precise than one long take.

A single full take also works; it just gets split and lined up.

**ElevenLabs starting point:**
- Stability ~50
- Similarity ~75
- Style ~20
- Speed 0.95–1.0
